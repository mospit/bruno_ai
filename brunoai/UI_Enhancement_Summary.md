## 🎭 **Personality System Details**

### **Bruno's Emotional States**
1. **Friendly** (Default): Welcoming smile, neutral brown color
2. **Excited**: Wide smile, orange/amber color, bouncing animation
3. **Thinking**: Focused expression, blue color, thought bubble
4. **Celebrating**: Big smile, green color, party emojis
5. **Concerned**: Worried expression, red color, warning indicators
6. **Helpful**: Supportive smile, purple color, thumbs up gesture
7. **Sleepy**: Drowsy expression, muted color, "zzz" indicator

- **Budget Status**: Bruno's mood reflects spending health
- **Shopping Success**: Celebration when good deals found
- **Warning States**: Concerned expression for overspending
- **Helpful Moments**: Supportive mood during guidance
- **Idle States**: Sleepy mood during inactivity
