# Bruno AI Website Development Brief

## Project Overview
Create a modern, engaging website for **Bruno AI** - a personal meal planning assistant with shopping superpowers. Bruno is a friendly bear character who helps users save money on groceries while planning delicious, budget-friendly meals.

## Brand Personality
- **Character**: Bruno - A friendly, street-smart bear with a New York accent
- **Personality**: Helpful, budget-conscious, enthusiastic about food and savings
- **Tone**: Casual, encouraging, slightly playful ("That's what I'm talkin' about!", "Bada-bing!")
- **Mission**: Making meal planning affordable and stress-free for families

## Core Features to Highlight
1. **AI-Powered Meal Planning**: Custom meal plans based on family size, budget, and dietary restrictions
2. **Smart Shopping Lists**: Automatically generated ingredient lists with price optimization
3. **Instacart Integration**: One-click grocery ordering and delivery
4. **Budget Optimization**: Real-time savings tracking and deal hunting
5. **Dietary Accommodations**: Support for 12+ dietary restrictions and preferences
6. **Family-Friendly**: Scales from 1-20 family members
7. **Store Flexibility**: Works with 8+ major grocery chains

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Busy families and individuals looking to save money on groceries
- **Secondary**: Health-conscious meal planners, budget-conscious millennials
- **Pain Points**: Meal planning takes too long, grocery bills are too high, food waste
- **Goals**: Save time, save money, eat better, reduce food waste
